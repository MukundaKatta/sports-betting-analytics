Understanding Sharp vs Public Money

Bet % (Public)

Percentage of total bets placed on each side. Dominated by recreational bettors who tend to bet favorites, overs, and popular teams.

Money % (Sharp)

Percentage of total dollars wagered on each side. Sharp bettors place fewer but larger bets, making the money % more indicative of informed opinion.

Divergence Signal

When 70% of bets are on Team A but 60% of money is on Team B, sharps are likely on Team B. This divergence is the strongest indicator of sharp action.

Reverse Line Movement

When the line moves toward the side getting fewer bets, it confirms that the money (sharp action) is driving the move, not the public.
